# Expenvisor Website Requirements

## Overview

This document outlines the complete content, functionality, and feature requirements for the Expenvisor marketing website. This is a design-agnostic specification focused on what needs to be built, not how it should look.

## Website Goals

- Showcase the Expenvisor AI expense tracker app
- Drive downloads from App Store and Play Store
- Provide required legal documentation for app store compliance
- Build credibility and trust with potential users
- Support app store optimization (ASO) efforts

## Brand Identity

- **App Name**: Expenvisor
- **Tagline**: "Your finances, flowing seamlessly"
- **Domain**: Expenvisor.com
- **Target Audience**: Tech-savvy individuals aged 18-45 who want smart expense tracking
